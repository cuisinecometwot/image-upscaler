FAQ

        
Is this image upscaler really free?

        
Yes. There is no account, no limit on the number of images and no watermark.

        
Are my images uploaded to a server?

        

          No. The AI model is downloaded once and runs on your own GPU through WebGPU (or on the CPU
          if WebGPU is not available). Images never leave your device.
        

        
What kind of images work best?

        

          Anime, manga, illustrations, game art and other drawn images. It uses the
          realesr-animevideov3 Real-ESRGAN model, which sharpens lines and removes blur
          and JPEG artifacts. Photos work too, but faces and fine textures can look painted.
        

        
How big can the output be?

        

          Up to about 80 megapixels (for example 2000×1500 → 8000×6000 at x4). Large images take
          longer, and phones may need a smaller input.
        

        
Which browsers are supported?

        

          Recent Chrome and Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android use the GPU and are fastest.
          Browsers without WebGPU fall back to the slower CPU mode.
        

        
Can it remove the background too?

        

          Use the free
          anime background remover
          to cut out characters to a transparent PNG, also in your browser with no upload.
